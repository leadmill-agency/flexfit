For First Timers

Never done Pilates? Perfect.

Beginners are the default at FlexFit. The reformer adjusts to you, not the other way around.

i.

The reformer adjusts.

Springs, straps, foot bars — every movement scales to your body. No prerequisites. No prior strength required.

ii.

Instructors guide.

Small groups, max 8. Every cue is for you, not the room. You'll never wonder if you're doing it right.

iii.

Slow is the default.

Pilates is control, not chaos. Low-impact strength built through repetition. No jumping, no shouting, no race.
